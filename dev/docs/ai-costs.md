## What AI costs (Claude)

Everything in JMG Ultra Studio works without AI. A key makes Story's themes (yours and the ones it finds, in one pass), Find more, the narrative cut and Moments smarter, and lets the Assistant understand anything. Only text is ever sent – transcript lines and your request, never footage.

**Measured on your FJL Summer 2026 project** (54 interviews, about 14 hours, 7,237 lines). These are estimates from the exact text Ultra Studio would send; answers are usually shorter than allowed for, so real costs tend to be a little lower.

| What you do | Opus 5.5 (best) | Asked again soon after | Sonnet 5.5 | Haiku 5.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Find themes (all interviews) | about 32¢ | about 6¢ | 16¢ | under 1¢ |
| Your own theme / Find more on a topic | about 14¢ | about 9¢ | 7¢ | under 1¢ |
| Narrative cut (any length) | about 28¢ | about 18¢ | 14¢ | under 1¢ |
| Moments – Only the best (120 checked) | about 15¢ | about 9¢ | 7¢ | under 1¢ |
| Moments – Balanced (240 checked) | about 31¢ | about 19¢ | 16¢ | under 1¢ |
| Moments – Everything useful (all 384) | about 51¢ | about 33¢ | 26¢ | about 1¢ |
| An Assistant request | 2–5¢ | | 1–3¢ | under 1¢ |

**Asking again soon after is cheaper.** Ultra Studio sends your transcripts in a form Claude can reuse – for an hour for narratives and the Assistant (which you tend to repeat), five minutes for the rest – which is what the "asked again" column shows.

**How much the AI reads.** Find themes, Add one more theme, Find more and the narrative cut each have a slider, from *Best 600* (or fewer) up to *Everything*. The table above is the default: the strongest 1,200 lines, so bigger projects cost about the same (your 30-hour BollagKremer project, 222 interviews: Find themes about 27¢). *Everything* sends every line, in batches, and the line under the slider shows the cost and time before anything runs. ### A typical project

For an FJL-sized documentary – two theme runs, ten own themes / Find more searches, six narrative drafts, one Moments pass and about fifty Assistant requests:
- **Claude Opus 5.5: about $5**
- Claude Sonnet 5.5: about $2.50
- Claude Haiku 5.5: about 15¢ (much weaker at judging story)

Using *Everything* for every search on a project that size comes to roughly $20–25 with Opus. Most of the benefit comes from one *Everything* pass of Find themes near the start; keep everyday searches at the default.

### Suggested budget

- Put **$25 of prepaid credit** on console.anthropic.com and set a **monthly spend limit of $50** there.
- In Ultra Studio › Settings › AI › Cost: keep **Ask before a request costing over 25¢** and set a **monthly limit** (e.g. $40) – Ultra Studio stops before going over it.
- Settings shows **this month's spending**, per feature, from Claude's own usage figures.
- Start with **Opus** for story work; switch to **Sonnet** if you want to halve the cost.

### Gemini instead

Your Google Gemini key works for all of this at no cost within Google's daily allowance (the themes step takes longer: up to a couple of minutes). When Google's main model is too busy to answer (it sometimes says "high demand"), Ultra Studio asks Google's quicker model instead, so the job still finishes; the log says when that happened. One thing to weigh for sensitive interviews: on Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products; Anthropic doesn't use API data for training by default. Choose in Settings › AI.
