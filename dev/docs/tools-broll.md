### B-roll – cover jump cuts

When you cut interviews down, the picture jumps wherever two pieces of the same interview meet. B-roll lays a cutaway over each join while the sound carries on, using the best moments from your B-roll – on a copy of the sequence, so your cut is never changed.

1. Open the sequence and check **Talking heads on** (usually V1). **Cover** chooses where cutaways go: **Jump cuts** (joins inside the same interview), **Cuts between people too** (also cuts back to someone you've already seen – a person is never covered the first time they appear) or **Long stretches** (a cutaway every 5–30 s in long talking heads, set with **A cutaway every**).
2. Set the **Cutaway length** and how long it **Starts before the cut**. The list shows every spot; click one to jump there. Spots that already have B-roll over them are left alone.
3. Under **B-roll clips**, pick a bin (or select clips in the Project panel). With **Pick the best moments with Selects** on, the B-roll is analysed like Selects does (**Kind of footage** starts on *Any footage*: only technically unusable moments are left out) and the sharpest, steadiest moments are used first, each only once and never the same clip twice in a row.
4. Press **Make a B-roll copy**. You get “*your sequence* – B-roll” with the cutaways on a new track called **B-roll**, picture only (switch on **Keep the B-roll's own sound** if you want it). The track is chosen so no interview or music is ever overwritten.

Works well after Story's narrative cut. The Assistant understands *“cover the jump cuts with B-roll”*.
