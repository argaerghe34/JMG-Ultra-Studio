### Assistant – just ask

Type what you want: *“cut the silences”*, *“markers on every beat”*, *“find lines about family”*, *“a 60-second video about volunteering”*, *“cover the jump cuts with B-roll”*, *“match the cameras”*, *“cut between the cameras”*, *“add captions”*, *“these clips are V-Log”*, *“check the cut”*, *“safe zones for Reels”*, *“export the master and a vertical version”*. It shows a **plan** first – each step in plain words, with the ones that change your project marked – and only acts when you press **Run**.

Common requests work with **no AI key**. With a key (Settings), it can handle anything phrased freely and chain several steps. It can only ever run Ultra Studio's own tools.

**My interviews** (needs AI): switch the Assistant to **My interviews** and ask anything about what your interviews say – *every story about Grandma, in the order it happened*, *where do the interviewees disagree?*, *a 45-second opening about why they came*, *who talks about their parents, and what do they say?*. In **Anything** mode such questions go there by themselves. The answer is a few sentences and the clips, in groups – each clip a whole stretch of speech, with a note on why it's there and its length; the total shows against the length you asked for. Nothing changes in Premiere until you choose: play, move, take out, rename or recolour, then **New sequence** (each group in turn, in its colour, with a marker on every clip, group names as markers or a name layer, optionally in the order they were filmed), **Mark a sequence** (range markers on a string-out or your cut), **Themes** (one per group) or **Word or text**. Type a change under the answer – *shorter*, *only Ruth*, *split it before and after the move* – and it answers again in full. It reads what you chose to work on in Story, and the answers are kept with the project (**Earlier** on the welcome).
