### Camera Switch – cut between interview angles

For interviews filmed on two or more cameras: it cuts between the angles at the ends of sentences, on a copy, without touching the sound.

1. Put the angles on top of each other, synced, on **V1**, **V2** (and **V3**…). Premiere can sync them: select the clips › right-click › **Synchronize**. Open that sequence – Camera Switch lists the angles under **Cameras**; switch off any you don't want, and they're left out of the copy.
2. Choose **Alternate angles** (one person: it goes round the cameras, starting on the one you pick in **Start on**) or **Follow who's speaking** (it shows each person's camera – say **Whose camera is whose** – keeps quick replies on the shot before, and cuts long answers to the wide and back).
3. Set the **Shortest shot** and **Longest shot**. The coloured strip shows the plan; click a shot to jump there.
4. Press **Make a camera cut copy**. You get “*your sequence* – camera cut”: the upper camera tracks are cut so the chosen angle shows, and the angle on the lowest track runs whole underneath, so you can fine-tune by trimming.

**Only one camera?** Camera Switch punches in instead: every other shot is zoomed (**Punch-in**, 105–140%, 115% to start), so a single-camera interview cuts like two angles.

With Premiere's transcripts read into Story, the cuts fall between sentences and it knows who is speaking; without them it cuts in the pauses (and alternates). The Assistant understands *“cut between the cameras”*.
